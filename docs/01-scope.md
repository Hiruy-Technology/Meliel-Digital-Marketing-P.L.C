# Contracted Scope

Source: Website Development Agreement HT-2026-006 (Articles 2 to 9), signed October 19, 2026.
The scope is **fixed**. Anything not listed here is out of scope unless Jacob says it has been agreed in writing.

## Pages (Article 2)

| Page | What it delivers |
|---|---|
| Home | Bold first impression: headline, featured work, services overview, client logos, key results in numbers, testimonials, clear calls to action |
| About Us | Meliel's story, mission and values, the team behind the work, and how Meliel works with its clients |
| Services | A dedicated section for each service Meliel offers, explaining what clients receive |
| Portfolio / Our Work | Visual gallery of past work; visitors filter projects by service or industry |
| Project Detail Pages | One page per project: the client, the challenge, what Meliel delivered (images and videos), and the results achieved |
| Contact / Start a Project | Short project inquiry form, plus phone, email, WhatsApp, Telegram, office location map, social media links |
| Supporting Pages | Thank-you confirmation page, friendly "page not found" page, standard Privacy Policy page |

## Features (Article 3)

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Portfolio Manager | Password-protected control panel where Meliel's team adds, edits, or removes portfolio projects, testimonials, and client logos at any time, with no coding |
| AI Case Study Writer | When adding a project, the team types short notes; AI drafts a polished description that the team reviews and edits before publishing |
| Filterable Portfolio | Filter by service or industry; full-screen image and video previews |
| Smart Inquiry Form | Asks for the service needed, budget range, and timeline |
| Email Notifications | Instant email alert to Meliel's team on every new inquiry |
| WhatsApp & Telegram Buttons | One-tap contact buttons on every page |
| SEO Optimized | Structured so Meliel can be found on Google for marketing services |
| Visitor Analytics | Visitor counts, pages viewed, traffic sources |
| Google Maps & Social Links | Embedded office map and links to all active social profiles |
| Fast Performance | Images and videos optimized for all connection types |
| Fully Responsive | Mobile, tablet, desktop |

## Technology (Article 4)

Next.js + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vercel or equivalent hosting, AI case study drafting. Equivalent or better technology may be used if deliverables are unaffected.

## Commercial terms that affect the build

- **Revisions**: 2 rounds **total**, consolidated feedback, submitted within 5 business days of each deliverable.
- **Third-party costs** (domain, hosting, database, storage, email service, AI after month 2) are paid by Meliel. Hiruy covers AI API costs for the first 2 months after launch.
- **Accounts**: Jacob registers all services under a dedicated Meliel-owned email, then hands everything over with a written guide.
- **Delivery**: Meliel reviews on staging, pays the 70% delivery payment, then the site launches and is handed over.
- **Support**: 2 to 3 months of free bug-fix support after launch. New features, redesigns, and content updates are not included.
- **IP**: transfers to Meliel on full payment. Hiruy may show the site in its portfolio. Third-party components must have licenses permitting commercial use.

## Out of scope (do not build without written approval)

- Blog or news section
- General page builder or editable page copy beyond the collections in CLAUDE.md
- Client login areas, payments, or e-commerce
- Social media feed integrations or auto-posting
- The **Meliel Management System** (a separate future project with its own agreement)
- Writing Meliel's content from scratch (Hiruy assists and polishes; Meliel provides)
